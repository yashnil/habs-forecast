Also in these waters, for sport shellfish harvest: the statewide mussel quarantine (CDPH SN26-008), and the Monterey County bivalve warning, which CDPH appears to have lifted (SN26-021, original SN26-018). Neritic treats it as neither in effect nor lifted until a person checks.

A notice missing here does not mean the water is open or the seafood safe.
