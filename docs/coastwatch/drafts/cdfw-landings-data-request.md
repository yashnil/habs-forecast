# DRAFT — not sent

**To:** CDFW Marine Region, Data Management (via the contact listed on the Marine Fisheries Data Explorer page, https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/Data-Management-Research/MFDE)
**Subject:** Request for publicly releasable annual commercial landings by port area and species, and permission to display them

Dear Marine Region Data Management team,

I maintain CoastWatch, a free, non-commercial website (no accounts, no advertising) that helps California's fishing communities follow official harmful-algal-bloom information. It shows CDFW and CDPH notices first, alongside NOAA's C-HARM forecast and CalHABMAP shore-station measurements.

We would like to show **historical fisheries exposure**: the past landed value of species affected by domoic-acid closures and advisories (Dungeness crab, rock crabs, northern anchovy, spiny lobster, Pacific sardine, bivalves). This would be shown by port area. It is labelled as history, never as predicted loss. Today we show only NOAA FOSS statewide totals, because port-area values for 2021 onward appear to be available only through the MFDE dashboards. We have not extracted any MFDE data.

We are asking:

1. **Data.** Is there a disclosure-safe, publicly releasable table of annual commercial landings (pounds and ex-vessel value) by **port area** and **species or market category**, for 2015 to the latest finalized year? A file you already publish, or an export of MFDE's public aggregates, would be ideal.
2. **Confidentiality.** How are confidential values marked (for example withheld cells or combined categories)? Which aggregation rules should we follow so that we never reveal a withheld value? This includes not deriving one by subtracting from a total. We already carry suppression through every sum, treat a missing value as "withheld" rather than zero, and never estimate suppressed cells.
3. **Permission.** May CoastWatch display these aggregates and keep a cached copy? We refresh automatically about weekly and keep previous versions for audit. If so, what attribution and disclaimer wording do you require? Are there conditions such as review before publication, update cadence, or removal on request?
4. **Revisions.** How are figures for recent years revised, and when are they considered final?

What we would publish: annual port-area × species-group totals, inflation-adjusted with the BLS CPI-U and clearly labelled, with the source, retrieval date and CDFW's attribution. We would not publish vessel, dealer or trip-level data.

Until we hear from you, port-level values stay marked "unavailable" on the site.
