Neritic · final logo comparison: R2 Bight against C2 Chart label

One decision board, no new variations. Both candidates are the round-2 masters exactly as committed (round2/svg, and the hand-drawn 16/24 px favicons in round2/icons). Every interface view is a Chromium screenshot of a faithful copy of the v4 TopBar, NavTabs, notices and status pills, using the env-chart (light reading) and env-instrument (dark map) tokens from src/v4/theme.css. The lockup sits where <Lockup size={30}> sits today; the phone bar shows the 32 px mark alone.

Honesty notes. The dark map behind the floating bar is an illustrative bathymetric backdrop drawn for this board (a running v4 map could not be captured here): it is not a screenshot and not data. 2x views are separate device-scale-2 renders shown pixel for pixel; 8x and 4x views are nearest-neighbour enlargements, so each square is one screen pixel. The glare row adds a uniform white veil (45 %) as a crude stand-in for sun on a phone screen. Nothing on this board is installed in the app.

1 · Desktop top bar

Explore floats the bar over the dark map; My Coast, Research and the reference pages put it in a band on the light reading environment. 1280 px wide, true size.

2 · Phone: top bar (mark in a round button) and bottom dock, 390 px

True size first, then the same screens rendered at device scale 2 (a phone's real pixel density), shown pixel for pixel. The dock is the same for both candidates (no mark), shown for context. My Coast's dock uses the dark instrument tokens even on the light page, as Briefing.tsx does.

3 · Favicons: 16 · 24 · 32 px in Chrome-like tab strips

16 and 24 px are the hand-drawn masters, as they would ship in favicon.ico (with no SVG favicon declared, because Chrome and Firefox prefer an SVG when both exist); 32 px and every retina slot are the favicon SVG. Neritic is the fifth tab, inactive, among eight deliberately hard generic neighbours.

4 · App icon

The square master (*-app.svg) at 512 (shown at half size) and 180 px, with the masks platforms apply: iOS rounded corners, a full circle, and the maskable-icon safe zone (Android may keep only the centre 80 % circle). Bottom: the home-screen size, 60 px, at true size.

5 · One colour

Black on light and white on dark: the 16 px master, then the vector at 32 and 48 px; the 16 px master again at 8x. R2 is shown with its mandatory 1 px rim (without it the two solids read as a lowercase r: round-2 critique).

6 · Light and dark grounds

The symbol (48 px) and lockup (40 px tall) on the app's grounds. Light grounds take the light master; dark grounds take the round-2 dark master.
