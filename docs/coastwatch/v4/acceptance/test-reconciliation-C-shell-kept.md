### Structural facts behind the classifications

1. **Dead v2 components.** `components/map/Tour.tsx`, `components/entry/Welcome.tsx`, `components/LiveOceanMap.tsx` and `components/coast/MyCoast.tsx` are still in `src/`, but no route imports them. The tour and welcome are gone for users.
2. **Two entry points now lead nowhere:**
   - `/about` still shows "Take the tour of the Ocean Map →" (`src/app/about/page.tsx:36`, testid `about-tour`), linking to `/?tour=1`. Explore ignores `tour`.
   - The About purpose switch still has "Show the welcome again" (`src/components/welcome/PurposeSwitch.tsx:50-66`, testid `purpose-reset`). It goes to `/`, where there is no welcome.
3. **Two notice surfaces on the reference pages (/bloom, /fisheries, /sources, /about):**
   - The v4 `notices-button` and `notices-panel` (`src/v4/shell/Notices.tsx`).
   - The v2 `official-drawer`, mounted by `AppShell.tsx:43` and opened from the status line's "See the list" (`components/Banners.tsx:77,85`) and from Fisheries (m7.spec.ts:131).
   - Explore, My Coast and Research have only the v4 panel.
4. **What the v4 notices panel lacks compared with the v2 drawer:**
   - No sentence saying a missing notice "does not mean an area is open".
   - No `tel:` agency phone lines.
   - No modal focus handling and no focus return: Escape just runs `setOpen(false)` (`Notices.tsx:23-29`).
   - My Coast prints the agency phone numbers as plain text, not `tel:` links (`v4/coast/Briefing.tsx:396`).
5. **"Research preview / not an official source" wording.**
   - Reference pages: in the footer (`AppShell.tsx:66-71`).
   - Explore, My Coast and Research: only inside the closed More menu (`v4/shell/TopBar.tsx:50`), which is not in the DOM until opened. The My Coast footer has only the independence line (`Briefing.tsx:422`).
6. **The forecast caveat "A low probability does not mean an area is safe"** (`content/copy.ts:54`, `FORECAST_COPY.lowNotSafe`) appears only in dead v2 components (`map/LayerDock.tsx:327`, `map/Inspector.tsx:210`, `coast/CoastSections.tsx:529`). The v4 forecast legend (`v4/explore/LayerPanel.tsx:192-197`) leaves it out.
7. **Suspected layout bug, not run.** `notices-panel` is `absolute right-0 w-[min(420px,100vw-24px)]`, anchored to the notices button. On phones the compact status centre (44 px) and More menu (44 px) sit to the right of the button. By arithmetic the panel's left edge lands about 90 px off-screen at 320 and 390 px. No test opens the panel on a phone.
