- the v4–v10 work: #68, #73, #75, #77, #79, #80 and #82;

## How main was integrated

The PRs were squash-merged in stack order: #68 → #73 → #75 → #77 → #79 → #80 → #82, then #84.
- After each merge, `main`'s tree was byte-identical to that PR's CI-green head.
- For each child PR, one `-s ours` merge of `main` recorded the parent's squash without changing the child's tree.
- No pushed branch was rewritten.
- Rollback for `main`: `archive/main-before-68` (`b78c69f`).

CI on `8631d0d8c`: all 6 checks green ([run 39998350383](https://github.com/yashnil/habs-forecast/actions/runs/39998350383)). One job needed a rerun after a Google Fonts fetch failed during the build.

## What this release does not include

- **Data PRs #72 (SF Bay satellite scope) and #74 (derived layers).** Neither is merged nor published. Production datasets were not changed.
- **Staging-only layers.** v1 therefore offers no gap-filled chlorophyll estimate, 8-day outlook or SST. The app reads `derived/index.json` (404 on production) and offers nothing derived. SF Bay chlorophyll stays cropped to the old scope.
- **Brand.** No new identity is installed; the current mark is unchanged.

## Known and open

- **Official notices:** not verified (`pending_human_review`). `cwp review-official` was not run; only a person may run it.
- **Data refresh:** some scheduled runs fail at the HF-radar cross-check against UCSD's ERDDAP (3 of the last 12). Publishing is blocked when that happens, the last good data stays live with its own dates, and the app says how old it is.
- **Owner decisions still open:**
  - brand identity;
  - economics choices D1–D5 and the exact CDFW recreational sale rule;
  - publishing #72/#74.
- **Known limits:**
  - Explore's JS bundle (~774 KB gzip) is over its 700 KB budget.
  - On a phone the briefing runs past 3 screens.
  - Safari and real devices are untested.
  - `tests/published/published.spec.ts` uses pre-v2 selectors and is stale; this release used the smoke checks above instead.
  - CI e2e jobs occasionally fail when the browser crashes on a runner.
