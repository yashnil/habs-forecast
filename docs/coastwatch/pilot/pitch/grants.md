# Funding paths (checked on funders' own sites, 2026-11-08/09)

**The short version for Thursday.** Nothing large is open to a student lead. Both Woods programs require an Academic Council or Medical Center Line faculty PI and both say applications are "expected to reopen in November", so the concrete ask is: *would you PI, or co-PI, a Big Ideas for Oceans or EVP proposal when the call opens?* The only student-led option open now is the Mel Lane grant (due Nov 29).

"Not confirmed" means the funder's page did not state it on the day it was read. Re-check a row before citing it.

## Dropped
- **NSF CIVIC Innovation Challenge**: NSF marks the program archived (page updated Jan 20, 2026). Do not cite it.
- **NOAA IOOS Ocean Technology Transition**: FY26 closed in July 2026; CeNCOOS lists no small-grant call. Worth an email to CeNCOOS about teaming, not a grant to name.

No program here funds an individual directly without a host institution. Do not apply anywhere without the PI's agreement.
