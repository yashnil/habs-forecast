# Neritic — coastal status for California fishermen (pilot)

**Problem.** Bloom and whale rules decide fishing days, but the facts are spread across CDPH PDFs, CDFW decisions, NWS and NOAA models. Fishermen asked for "a timely, accessible, and easily understood notification system" (Pomeroy & Culver 2026, 20 CA crab fishermen). This season commercial Dungeness is delayed to at least Jan 1, 2027 by whale entanglements, not toxin (CDFW, via plan §1.1).

**Product.** "Today at your port": official status, crab toxin tests, sea and weather, a 0–8 day bloom timeline, whale rules, what changed, offline save, alerts, and a cited assistant.

**Honesty.** Official status → measurements → forecasts → advice. No green "safe". Unreviewed notices say "Not verified". Chlorophyll is never presented as toxin.

**Science.** Days 0–3: NOAA C-HARM. Days 4–8: chlorophyll outlook (climatology + AR(1)), held-out RMSE 0.760 vs 0.857 climatology and 0.892 persistence, n = 185,364 (2019–21). The author's PINN matches, does not beat, a ConvLSTM; an internal audit suggesting a simple baseline matches both awaits independent reproduction.

**Pilot.** Moss Landing, Monterey, Santa Cruz, Half Moon Bay; 10 dockside interviews; free public-good tool. SMS in carrier review.

**Asks.** Methods critique and shared baseline · faculty PI on a Big Ideas for Oceans proposal · SUNTANS/SFEI data and validated Bay HAB maps · introductions (SFEI, CeNCOOS, UCSC C-HARM, CA Sea Grant, MLML/Hopkins) · a student research role.
