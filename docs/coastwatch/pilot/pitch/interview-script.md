# Dockside discovery interview (commercial fishermen, ~15 min)

**Consent (read aloud):** "I'm a student building a free tool for fishermen about closures, toxin tests and conditions. Can I ask you about 15 minutes of questions? I'll take notes, no recording unless you say yes, and I won't use your name or boat name. You can stop any time." Record: yes / no / notes-only.

Ask open-ended; do not demo before Q9.

1. What do you fish, out of which port, on what size boat?
2. Walk me through the morning you decide whether to go out. What do you check, in what order?
3. Where do you hear about closures, delays or toxin results? (CDFW, CDPH, hotline, buyers, other boats, radio)
4. Tell me about the last time a closure or rule change surprised you. What did it cost you?
5. Which sources do you trust, and which do you double-check? Why?
6. What makes an alert useful? What makes one annoying?
7. How much signal do you have offshore? Do you ever need information without coverage?
8. Text message, app, or something else? Would you install anything?
9. (Show Today screen, 1 min.) What's the first thing you look at? What's missing or wrong?
10. Would anything here make you act differently on a trip?
11. What would make you stop using it?
12. Who else should I talk to?

Do not ask about price or promise features.
