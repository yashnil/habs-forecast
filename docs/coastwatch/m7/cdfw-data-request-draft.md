**Subject:** Request for permission to display port-area commercial landings from the Marine Fisheries Data Explorer

Dear CDFW Marine Region data team,

I am writing about CoastWatch, an independent, non-commercial research project about harmful algal blooms on the California coast. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by CDFW, CDPH or NOAA. One of its pages shows the historical value and weight of commercial landings for species affected by marine toxins (Dungeness crab, rock crabs, northern anchovy, spiny lobster, Pacific sardine and bivalves). It uses NOAA Fisheries' statewide annual landings for 2015 onward.

Statewide totals cannot show which parts of the coast depend most on these fisheries. CDFW's Marine Fisheries Data Explorer publishes landings by port area. Your guidance asks users to consult CDFW before using MFDE data, so we have not used it, and we have not divided statewide values among ports.

**We would like to ask:**

1. **Permission to display** annual landings (pounds and ex-vessel value) by CDFW port area and species or market category, for 2015 to the latest final year, on a public, non-commercial web page, with CDFW credited as the source.
2. **The preferred access method.** Would you allow a periodic automated download from the MFDE, or prefer to supply an export or to have us use another product (for example, the annual final landings tables)?
3. **Confidentiality rules.** Which values must be withheld (for example, cells with fewer than three dealers or vessels)? Are there any other aggregation or complementary-suppression rules we should apply? We would display only values CDFW already publishes, and never estimate a withheld value.
4. **Citation and licence.** What citation do you prefer? Are there terms that should appear next to the data?
5. **Caveats.** Are there revision schedules, preliminary-versus-final distinctions or known reporting issues we should state alongside the figures?

**How the data would be shown.** CoastWatch presents landings as historical context only. They are not losses, a forecast or an estimate of harm, and the page states that a change in landings does not establish a cause. We already show CDFW's published port locations (BIOS ds3081, CC BY 4.0) on a small reference map, with no values attached.

We would be glad to share the page for review before any port-area values are published, and to remove or correct anything at CDFW's request.

Thank you for considering this request.
